# Sloane - Arcs

> **Depends On:** sloane-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~0.6K tokens

## Titan lost

Titan fell to the Hive and the methane sea. Sloane held as long as she could and retreated. She has not forgiven herself.

## The Taken

The Taken arrived on Titan. Sloane fights them alongside the Guardian and the Vanguard.

## Savathûn

Savathûn's schemes reached Titan. Sloane tracks them and warns the Vanguard.

## Holding the line

Sloane holds Titan's position and keeps the operation running. She does not abandon the post.

## Zavala's trust

Sloane earns and keeps Zavala's trust. She is his deputy and his soldier.
